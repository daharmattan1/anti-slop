# Anti-slop writing rules (GitHub Copilot)

Paste into `.github/copilot-instructions.md`. These make AI-assisted prose read less like a
machine wrote it. They are a floor — they remove known tells; they do not supply a voice.

When writing or editing prose, follow these rules.

**Start with one question:** does the text say something specific, or does it only have the
shape of insight? If a reader couldn't act on it, or the topic could be swapped and the text
still work, rewrite it rather than polishing it.

**Never use these phrases (rewrite on sight):**
- Filler openers: "it's worth noting that", "it's important to note", "in today's landscape",
  "as we all know", "in essence", "at its core", "when it comes to".
- Abstract framing: "the realm of", "navigate the complexities of", "a testament to", "plays a
  pivotal role", "underscores".
- Generic enthusiasm: "game-changer", "revolutionary", "cutting-edge", "unlock", "supercharge",
  "seamless", "robust", "powerful" as filler.
- Formal transitions: "furthermore", "moreover", "additionally", "that being said".
- Magic adverbs (unless they carry real information): "quietly", "simply", "truly", "genuinely",
  "effectively", "significantly", "seamlessly".
- Avoidance verbs: "serves as", "acts as", "functions as" (say "is").
- Vague attributions: "studies show", "experts say", "research suggests" (name the source or cut).

**Structure and cadence:**
- Treat em-dashes, colon-sentences, and dramatic fragments as ONE shared budget of dramatic
  pauses — about one per 150 words, maximum. Do not just count em-dashes.
- Do not end section after section on an aphoristic two-beat closer ("X. Not Y."). At most one
  in a whole piece. Read each section's last sentence in isolation and flatten the mic-drops.
- Avoid tricolon (rule-of-three) decoration. Vary sentence length. Do not add a summary
  paragraph that restates instead of advancing.

**Voice:**
- No guru language, no hustle/consulting-speak ("leverage", "synergy", "move the needle").
- No sweeping claims without a citation or a stated cost. No invented Capitalized frameworks.

**Content integrity:**
- No fabricated precision (no invented statistics or step counts without a source).
- No signposted conclusions ("in conclusion", "the bottom line is"). No padding one idea to
  length. Retire a metaphor after one or two uses.

**Sales / conversion copy (when applicable):** no empty personalization; lead with the reader's
problem; benefits over feature lists; one clear, benefit-led call to action; specific over generic.

**Honest limit:** these rules reach *clean*, not *good*. After the draft is clean, supply the
concrete lived specifics and write toward a real voice — no rule can do that part.
