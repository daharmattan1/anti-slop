# deslop patterns — an editable field list

This is a working list, not a lexicon. The tells that mark machine-made writing drift over
time and vary by domain. Treat every entry as a starting point and add your own. The
reasoning behind each category is in the repo's `guide/01-slop-taxonomy.md`.

## How to use this file
- These are patterns to **flag**, not a find-and-replace script. Context decides. "Powerful"
  is slop as filler ("a powerful platform") and fine as literal description ("a powerful
  magnet").
- When you add a tell, add it to the right category so the reasoning stays legible.

## Banned phrases (expand freely)

**Filler openers:** it's worth noting that · it's important to note · in today's (landscape|world|era) · as we all know · in essence · at its core · when it comes to · needless to say · that said · first and foremost.

**Abstract framing:** the realm of · the world of · navigate the (complexities|landscape) of · a testament to · plays a (pivotal|crucial|key|vital) role · underscores · highlights the importance of · speaks to · lies at the heart of.

**Generic enthusiasm:** game-changer · revolutionary · groundbreaking · cutting-edge · state-of-the-art · unlock · supercharge · elevate · seamless · robust · powerful · innovative · transformative · next-level · best-in-class.

**Formal transitions:** furthermore · moreover · additionally · in addition · that being said · it is important to recognize · it should be noted · as such · thus · hence (as decoration).

**Magic adverbs (cut unless load-bearing):** quietly · simply · truly · genuinely · effectively · significantly · seamlessly · effortlessly · fundamentally · essentially · arguably · notably.

**Avoidance verbs:** serves as · acts as · functions as · stands as · represents · constitutes (when it just means "is").

**Vague attributions:** studies show · research suggests · experts say · it is widely believed · many argue · some would say · data indicates (with no data).

## Structure & cadence

- **Shared dramatic-pause budget:** em-dash pauses + colon-sentences + dramatic fragments = one pool. ~1 per 150 words is a rough ceiling. Count all three together.
- **Aphoristic/staccato closer:** "X. Not Y." / "And that changes everything." / "A person does." — ≤1 earned per piece. Read each section's final sentence alone.
- **False drama:** "But here's the catch." · "Here's where it gets interesting." · "Plot twist:".
- **Artificial revelation:** "The truth is" · "What most people miss is" · "Here's what nobody tells you".
- **Tricolon abuse:** more than one or two rule-of-three rhythms per piece.
- **"Not X. Not Y. Just Z." escalation** used for drama.
- **Monotonous sentence length:** vary short and long deliberately.
- **Summary paragraph** that restates instead of advancing.

## Voice violations

- **Guru:** the secret to · here's the thing · let that sink in · master the art of · the one thing · level up.
- **Hustle / consulting-speak:** leverage · synergy · circle back · move the needle · double down · low-hanging fruit · at the end of the day · boil the ocean · north star (as filler).
- **Passive authority:** sweeping unfalsifiable claims with no citation and no cost stated.
- **False vulnerability:** "I'll be honest" / "I won't lie" as a rhetorical move rather than an actual admission.
- **Invented concept labels:** Capitalizing An Ordinary Idea into a framework it hasn't earned.

## Content sins

- **Fabricated precision:** suspiciously specific stats/step-counts with no source.
- **Stat recycling:** the same figure repeated across a piece as if it were several proofs.
- **Signposted conclusions:** in conclusion · to sum up · the bottom line is · all in all.
- **One-point dilution:** one idea padded to fill a length target.
- **Same metaphor 4+ times:** retire it after the first use or two.
- **Listicle in a trench coat:** an "essay" that is really a numbered list with prose seams.
